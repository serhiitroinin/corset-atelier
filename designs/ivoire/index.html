N°04

What you do not see

Most of a corset is hidden. We spend our time there. Eleven hours for a Séverine; twenty-six for a Colombe.

  1. The bones

    Spiral steel at the curves, flat steel at the back. Each one tipped, filed, and cased in its own channel so it never finds the cloth.

  2. The busk

    A German steel busk, five or six loops, set by hand. It closes with one quiet sound and stays closed.

  3. The waist tape

    A band of grosgrain stitched inside at the narrowest point. It takes the strain, so the seams need not.

  4. The layers

    Coutil for strength, a fashion cloth for the eye, cotton lawn against the skin. Cut on grain. Always.

  5. The finishing

    Binding felled by hand. Two-part eyelets, set one at a time. A number, written in ink, inside the lining.

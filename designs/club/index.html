What's inside

Fast-fashion "corsets" are a tank top with plastic straws in it. This is not that. Receipts below.

Receipts

  1. Spiral steel bones

    10 to 24 per piece. They bend with you when you dance and spring back after. Plastic bones warp by midnight. Ours don't.

  2. Steel busk

    The front clasp. Five loops, five pins, on in 20 seconds in a club bathroom. Tested. Extensively.

  3. Waist tape

    A hidden strip of twill at the narrowest point takes all the tension, so seams don't pop and the shape doesn't stretch out.

  4. 2–3 real layers

    Fashion fabric outside, cotton coutil as the strength layer, soft cotton lining against skin. Breathable. Not sweaty polyester.

  5. Finished by hand

    Binding, eyelets and bone tips are closed by a human in our atelier. Every piece is signed inside. Say hi to Olena.

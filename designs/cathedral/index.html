IV

Built like a vault. Worn like a vow.

Thirty-one hours, four layers, one maker from first chalk line to last knot. Nothing here is glued, fused, or plastic.

  1. The bones24 to 32 spiral steel bones flex with the breath; flat steels flank the eyelets so the back never buckles.
  2. The buskA 12 mm stainless spoon busk, wider at the belly, fastened with five hand-set loops and studs.
  3. The waist tapeHerringbone cotton tape stitched into every seam at the waistline. It carries the tension so the fabric never has to.
  4. The layersVelvet, brocade, damask, satin or leather face, fused to nothing; English cotton coutil strength layer; floating cotton lining dyed to match the glass.
  5. The finishingBone channels topstitched in silk thread, leather binding slip-stitched by hand, two-part eyelets set one at a time.

The fit finder

Sign up for your size.

Corsets are sized by their closed waist in inches, smaller than you are, on purpose. Fill in your dance card and we'll do the arithmetic.

How to measure: soft tape, bare skin, breathe out normally. Waist is the narrowest point, usually two fingers above the navel. Underbust is where a bra band sits.

Inside the satin

Soft to look at. Not soft at all.

  1. Spiral steel bones12 to 24 per corset. They bend with you when you sit, reach and dance, then spring back.
  2. A steel busk, or noneSpoon-smooth front fastening with pearl-tone loops. Ribbon-front styles skip it for a closed, balletic line.
  3. Waist tapeA hidden grosgrain band at the waistline takes the strain, so the shape you buy is the shape you keep.
  4. Three layersSatin face, cotton coutil strength layer, brushed cotton lining against your skin.
  5. Finished by handBinding, eyelets and bows are set by hand in our studio. About nine hours per piece.

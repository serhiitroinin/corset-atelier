The fitting journey

Twelve weeks, gently kept

You will always know what comes next. We hold the calendar, so that you need not.

  1. 12 weeks before

    Begin

    Send your measurements and wedding date. We reserve your place in the workroom.

  2. Week 11

    Consultation

    An hour together with silks, laces and sketches. Your design is agreed and drawn.

  3. Week 7

    Toile fitting

    A cotton toile, boned and laced on you. Every seam is pinned and corrected.

  4. Week 2

    Final lacing

    Your finished bodice. We teach whoever will lace you on the morning itself.

  5. The day

    Yours

    Pressed, boxed in tissue, with spare laces and our telephone number, just in case.

Journal · Inside the bodice

What holds you, quietly

A bridal corset must do its work for fourteen hours and never once be noticed. This is how ours are made.

  1. Spiral and flat steel bonesSpiral steel flexes with you at the sides; flat steel keeps the back lacing perfectly straight.
  2. A silk-covered buskThe front closure, wrapped by hand so no metal shows against the silk.
  3. The waist tapeA hidden band of herringbone twill that takes the strain, so seams never do.
  4. Three layersSilk outside, English cotton coutil for strength, fine cotton lawn against the skin.
  5. Hand finishingBound edges, flossed bone ends, and your initials and date stitched inside in pale blue.

If your bodice does not fit as it should, we alter or remake it at no cost. Ready-cut pieces exchange freely within 30 days.

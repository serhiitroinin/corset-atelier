Section the Fourth · From the workshop journal

What is Inside it

A corset is judged by the parts nobody sees. Here they are, so you may judge.

  1. The bones

    Sprung spiral steel on the curves, flat steel at the back beside the grommets. Tipped, not merely cut. They bend with you and return; plastic does neither.

  2. The busk

    A half-inch stainless busk with five loops and posts, set by hand so it closes flush. Spoon busks on the overbusts, for the lower stomach’s sake.

  3. The waist tape

    An inch of twill tape runs inside the narrowest line and takes the strain, so the seams need not. It is why our waists measure in year five what they did in week one.

  4. The layers

    Fashion cloth, herringbone coutil for strength, cotton lining against the skin. Three layers, each cut on grain. We do not fuse; glue is for envelopes.

  5. The hand finishing

    Binding is felled by hand, casings are flossed at the ends, and every grommet is set two-part with a washer. Then somebody else checks it, grudgingly.

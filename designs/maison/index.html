N°05

What you do not see

Most of a corset is hidden. Ours is built the way corsets at three times the price are built. Steel throughout, and tested for 200 wears.

  1. The bones

    Spiral steel at the curves, flat steel at the back. No plastic, anywhere. Each bone tipped and cased in its own channel so it never finds the cloth.

  2. The busk

    A steel busk, five or six loops. It closes with one quiet sound and stays closed.

  3. The waist tape

    A band of grosgrain stitched inside at the narrowest point. It takes the strain, so the seams need not.

  4. The layers

    Coutil for strength, satin or sateen for the eye, cotton against the skin. Cut on grain. Always.

  5. The test

    Two-part eyelets that do not pull through. Bound edges, stitched flat. Every style is laced, worn and unlaced 200 times before it goes on sale.

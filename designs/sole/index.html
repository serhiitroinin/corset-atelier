What's inside

Friendly outside. Serious engineering inside.

A corset that fits well feels like a firm hug, not a punishment. That's down to five boring, brilliant things we refuse to skimp on — in a size 18 or a size 40.

  1. Spiral steel bones12 to 28 of them. They flex with you when you sit, bend and reach for the top shelf. More bones as sizes go up, so support never thins out.
  2. A steel buskThe front fastening. Five loops, five pins, on in ten seconds. No wrestling a zip behind your back.
  3. Waist tapeA hidden twill ribbon that takes all the strain, so the seams don't. It's why the curve holds for years.
  4. Three layersCotton coutil for strength, a fashion fabric for looks, soft cotton lining against your skin. Breathable. Not sweaty.
  5. Hand finishingBinding stitched and eyelets set by hand in our Valencia workroom, by people paid properly to do it.

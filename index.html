Research behind the directions ↓

01 · Market

Corsets left the costume box

  • Market estimated at $1.2–1.8B (2025), growing 6–9% a year; bridal and outerwear styling drive it.
  • Styling moved from drama to daywear: corset + tailoring, shirts, denim.
  • Aesthetic engines: regencycore, gothic revival, balletcore/coquette, office siren, bridal, Westwood-archive nostalgia.
  • Underserved and loyal: plus-size and larger-cup buyers, first-timers afraid of fit.
02 · References

Who owns which corner

  • Couture: Vivienne Westwood, Dilara Findikoglu, Schiaparelli
  • Fashion-forward: Miaou, House of CB, Bordelle, Agent Provocateur
  • Heritage and bespoke: Vollers (1899), Dark Garden, What Katie Did
  • Volume and fit-first: Orchard Corset, Corset Story
  • Alt and romance: Killstar, Son de Flor, indie Etsy stay-makers
03 · UX that sells corsets

Fit risk is the blocker

  • Size by waist measurement, never dress size; show expected reduction.
  • Construction spec block: bone count and type, layers, busk, waist tape.
  • Reviews that carry the reviewer's measurements and size bought.
  • Made-to-measure flow with lead time; lacing and seasoning guides.
  • Every direction here ships a working fit finder for that reason.
